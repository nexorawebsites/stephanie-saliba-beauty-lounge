Help & Info

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our handmade crochet creations

Do you make custom orders?

+

Absolutely! We love bringing your creative visions to life. You can choose your preferred colors, size, style, and even send us inspiration photos. Simply fill out our Custom Order form or message us on WhatsApp to get started. We'll work closely with you to create a one-of-a-kind piece that's perfect for your needs.

How long does production take?

+

Production time varies depending on the complexity and size of the item. Simple items like keychains typically take 2-3 days, while larger pieces like bouquets or custom plushies can take 5-10 business days. During peak seasons (holidays, Valentine's Day, etc.), we recommend ordering at least 2 weeks in advance. We'll always keep you updated on your order's progress!

Do you offer gift wrapping?

+

Yes! We offer beautiful premium gift wrapping and packaging options. Each item can be carefully wrapped in our signature packaging, making it ready to gift directly to your loved ones. We can also include a personalized message card. Simply let us know when placing your order if you'd like the gift wrapping option.

What payment methods are accepted?

+

We accept various payment methods for your convenience including cash on delivery, bank transfer, and WhatsApp Pay. For international orders, we can discuss the most convenient payment option for you. Just reach out to us via WhatsApp and we'll accommodate your preferred payment method.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes, we offer international shipping! Shipping rates and delivery times vary depending on the destination. Please contact us on WhatsApp for a shipping quote to your location. We ensure all items are carefully packaged to arrive safely at your doorstep, no matter where you are in the world.

How do I care for my crochet items?

+

Our crochet items are made with high-quality yarns that are easy to care for. For most items, we recommend gentle hand washing with mild soap and laying flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting. For display pieces like flowers and bouquets, simply dust them gently or use a soft brush. Each order comes with specific care instructions.
